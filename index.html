Practice Exam

PMP® Exam Sample Questions

Test yourself with practice questions covering risk, schedule, EVM, quality, conflict resolution, and team dynamics — written in the style of real PMP® exam scenarios.

Question 01 · Risk Management

During the risk management process, an insurance policy was purchased to reduce the impact of an identified risk. Which risk response strategy is this?

Transferring the financial responsibility or impact of a risk to a third party (such as an insurance company) is the Transfer strategy.

Question 02 · Conflict Resolution

Two team members disagree about task allocation. The project manager listens to both sides and works toward a mutually acceptable solution. Which conflict resolution technique is this?

Combining both parties' viewpoints to reach a win-win outcome and consensus is the Collaborate technique — the most effective, though most time-consuming, method.

Question 03 · Earned Value Management

A project has EV (Earned Value) = $4,000, PV (Planned Value) = $5,000, and AC (Actual Cost) = $4,500. What is the project's Cost Performance Index (CPI), and where does it stand against budget?

CPI = EV / AC = 4000 / 4500 = 0.89. Since CPI is below 1, the project is spending more than planned — it is over budget.

Question 04 · Stakeholder Management

The project manager prepares a stakeholder engagement matrix by analyzing stakeholders' expectations, interest levels, and influence over the project. Which knowledge area does this activity belong to?

Classifying stakeholders by their level of influence and interest, then defining engagement strategies, is a core output of the Stakeholder Management knowledge area.

Question 05 · Integrated Change Control

During a project, the customer requests an additional feature that was not part of the original contract. What should the project manager do first?

Any request outside the agreed scope must go through the formal Integrated Change Control process so its impact can be assessed before it is approved and implemented.

Question 06 · Team Development

In a project team, new members are still getting to know each other and roles are not yet clearly defined. According to Tuckman's stages of team development, which stage is this?

In the Forming stage, team members get acquainted, roles are still unclear, and the team relies heavily on the leader for direction.

Question 07 · Estimating

When estimating how long a task will take, the project manager calculates a weighted average using the most pessimistic, most likely, and most optimistic durations. Which estimating technique is this?

Three-Point (PERT) Estimating is calculated as a weighted average of the optimistic, pessimistic, and most likely durations: (O + 4M + P) / 6.

Question 08 · Quality Management

During quality control, a diagram resembling a "bone structure" is used to categorize and visualize the causes of product defects. Which tool is this?

The Fishbone (Ishikawa) Diagram is a quality tool that supports root cause analysis by grouping a problem's possible causes into categories.

Question 09 · Schedule Management

A project manager uses a Gantt chart to monitor progress. How does a delay in an activity on the critical path affect the project's finish date?

Activities on the critical path have zero total float, so any delay on these activities directly pushes back the project's finish date.
